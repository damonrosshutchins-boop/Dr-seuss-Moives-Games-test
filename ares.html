Ares

Opens a page in a tab whose address bar shows blob:null/…. The tab's title and icon are a disguise; the real page runs inside it.

Why the address is just blob:null/…: the button lives inside a sandboxed frame with allow-scripts allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox and no allow-same-origin, so that frame has an opaque origin. A Blob URL made there is blob:null/<id>, and the tab it opens inherits that opaque origin — the host that served this page never appears in the URL, the tab history, or the title. The opened document is only a shell with the disguise title and icon and one full-screen iframe pointing at the real address; the framed page cannot retitle the tab because it is cross-origin to it.

  • A click is required: browsers only allow window.open from a user gesture, which is why the launcher is a button.
  • The disguise hides the address, title and icon, not the traffic: devtools still shows the framed page's real hosts.
  • The blob:null tab itself has no storage of its own; the framed page keeps its own cookies and origin.
  • Sites that refuse framing (X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors) stay blank in the tab.
