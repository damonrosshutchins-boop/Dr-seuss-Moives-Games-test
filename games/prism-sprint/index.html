Prism Sprint

Three short courses, neon obstacles and collectible shards. Jump over the hazards to reach the exit.

Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W to jump, P to pause, R to restart. Touch: tap the stage or Jump.
This is an independently written game with original visuals and sound effects. It does not contain or redistribute Web Dashers, Geometry Dash, their levels, artwork or music. Play does not require an external server, Wisp relay, or an account.
